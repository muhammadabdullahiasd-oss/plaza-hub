Features
Everything you need to run your plaza

Purpose-built for Nigerian commercial plaza management — from single-block plazas to large multi-block properties.

Tenant Management

Register tenants, assign shop numbers, manage contact details, and keep full tenancy histories. Add or remove tenants with ease.

Rent Expiry Tracking

Automatic color-coded alerts for leases expiring soon. Never miss a renewal — get notified 30, 14, and 7 days in advance.

Online Payments

Tenants pay annual fees directly through the portal via Paystack or bank transfer. Auto-confirmation and instant receipts.

Revenue charts, occupancy rates, payment trends, and exportable reports. Know your plaza's financial health at a glance.

Smart Notifications

Bulk SMS and email reminders to tenants for renewals, overdue balances, and maintenance updates — sent automatically.

How It Works
Up and running in minutes

Getting started with PlazaHub is simple — no technical knowledge required.

1

Create Your Plaza Account

Sign up and set up your plaza profile — add your plaza name, address, blocks, and total units in a few clicks.

2

Register Your Tenants

Add each tenant to the system, assign them a shop, set the rent amount and payment period, and send them their login details.

3

Tenants Pay Online

Tenants log in to their personal portal to check their status, pay annual fees via Paystack, and download receipts.

4

You Monitor Everything

The admin dashboard gives you full visibility — payments, expiring leases, occupancy, and revenue — all in one place.
